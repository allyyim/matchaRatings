1. Introduction

Sip & Score ("App," "we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your personal data in connection with our matcha rating and review application.

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the App, including features used, search queries, and timestamps.
  • Device Information: Browser type, device type, operating system, and mobile network information.
  • Log Data: Server logs of your requests and API calls to our service.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google Authentication: When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google ID, name, and email address from Google.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App

  • Process and display your ratings and reviews
  • Enable social features (following users, viewing shared content)
  • Personalize your experience
  • Send transactional communications (e.g., account updates)
  • Analyze usage patterns and improve the App's functionality
  • Detect and prevent fraud or misuse
